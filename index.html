FAQ

Good to know.

Do I need a separate account for each app? +

No. One Ghostroute account automatically gives you access to all of Ghostroute's mini-app — all from the same login and the same balance.

Is my first search really free? +

Yes. Every paid tool gives you one free search with no card required to start. Bolt is unlimited and free at all times.

How do i contact Ghostroute's Support team? +

Simple. You can reach out to Ghostroute by sending an email to: ghostroute.security@gmail.com

How do payments and top-ups work? +

All top-ups and payments are processed securely through Paystack. You top up your balance from as little as ₦100 / $1, and each search deducts only its listed price — shown before you ever process it.

Can I cancel or stop using a tool anytime? +

There's nothing to cancel — there are no subscriptions. You only ever pay per search (or data), so you can stop, pause, or come back whenever you like.
