Payment FAQ

Good to know, before you deposit.

Does Ghostroute ever store my card? +

Never. Your card details never reach Ghostroute.
Every payment happens entirely inside Paystack's secure payment environment.

How do i contact Ghostroute's Support team? +

Simple. You can reach out to Ghostroute by sending an email to: ghostroute.security@gmail.com

Which currency should I deposit in? +

If you're in Nigeria, ₦ is usually simplest.
Everywhere else, $ works best.
Either way, your balance is converted fairly — you're never worse off for choosing one over the other.

How fast does my balance update? +

Immediately Paystack confirms your payment, Ghostroute checks that result and credits your balance automatically.

What if I close the Paystack window before finishing? +

No payment means no deduction. Your balance simply remains untouched.
You can simply start the deposit again from your dashboard.

Is Paystack safe to use? +

Yes. Paystack is a trusted, bank-grade payment processor used worldwide, with its own fraud and risk checks on every transaction. Ghostroute simply relies on their verified result.
